Saves your favourite destination permanently — even after closing the browser.

Session Storage Temporary

Stores your current trip notes temporarily — clears when you close the browser tab.

How to Use This Page

  • Drag & Drop: Click and hold a Travel Component, then drag it to the itinerary board on the right.
  • Local Storage: Type a preference name + value, click Save. Data stays even after closing the browser.
  • Session Storage: Type a note label + content, click Save. Data is removed when the tab closes.
  • Retrieve: Enter the same preference name/note label and click Retrieve to read the saved value.
  • Delete: Enter the key or leave blank and click Delete to remove one or all saved entries.
